# Claude Arcade

A little arcade of hand-made, single-file browser games — no installs, just click and play.

▶ **Play online:** https://richjackson.github.io/claude-arcade/

## Games

### 👾 Space Warriors
Pick a character, board your spaceship, and fly around a hand-drawn galaxy. Shoot
monsters, explore 18 named planets, and open presents — but not all gifts are good!
Walk around the ship interior to rest, train, and **cook ingredients into meals** that
grant different power-ups depending on the recipe.

**The goal:** hunt down the four ingredients for the legendary **Magic Custard Pie**
(biscuit, whipped cream, Birds custard powder & milk), hidden inside presents out in
space. Collect all four to **win** and throw a custard-pie party with all your friends! 🥧

- **Play:** https://richjackson.github.io/claude-arcade/space-warriors.html
- **Controls (keyboard):** Arrows / WASD = move · Space = shoot / use room · B = fly home · P = pause · M = mute
- **Controls (mobile):** on-screen D-pad to move, **FIRE** to shoot, **USE** for room actions, **HOME** to fly back, plus pause/mute — touch controls appear automatically on phones & tablets.

### 🏭 Factory Rangers
A big, cartoonish **twin-stick shooter** set inside a busy factory. Two hordes fight for
control: the **Rangers** (humans 🦸) and the **Goonies** (monsters 👹). Every shot that
lands on a member of the *other* side **flips them onto yours** — so the battle is a
chaotic tug-of-war. Each side wields its own selection of guns (blasters, spreaders &
rapid rifles), and even **you** can get zapped and switch teams!

**The goal:** wipe out a whole horde. Convert every Goonie and the Rangers win — but let
the monsters take over and it's game over. You're the strongest unit on the field, so
charge in and tip the balance.

- **Play:** https://richjackson.github.io/claude-arcade/factory-rangers.html
- **Controls (keyboard):** WASD / Arrows = move · Mouse = aim · Click or hold = shoot · P = pause · M = mute
- **Controls (mobile):** twin virtual sticks — **left** side of the screen to move, **right** side to aim & fire. Drag anywhere; the sticks appear automatically on phones & tablets.

## Running locally

Every game is a self-contained `.html` file. Just open it in a browser, or serve the folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server
# then visit http://localhost:8000/
```
